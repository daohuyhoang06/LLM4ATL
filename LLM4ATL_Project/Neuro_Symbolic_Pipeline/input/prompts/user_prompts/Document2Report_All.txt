Transform a model conforming to the Document metamodel into a model conforming to the Report metamodel. Name the ATL transformation Document2Report_All.

Each DocumentModel is transformed into a ReportModel.

The documents of the DocumentModel are copied to the reports of the ReportModel.

Each Document is transformed into a Report.

The title of the Document is copied to the title of the Report.

The author of the Document is copied to the author of the Report.

The sections of the Document are copied to the sections of the Report.

Each Section is transformed into a Section.

The title of the Section is copied to the title of the Section.

The content of the Section is copied to the content of the Section.

The order of the Section is copied to the order of the Section.

Transform a BibTeX bibliography into a DocBook document. Produce one DocBook containing one Book and one Article titled "BibTeXML to DocBook". The article must contain four first-level sections named "References List", "Authors list", "Titles List", and "Journals List".

Each BibTeXFile is transformed into a DocBook.

Each Author is transformed into a Para.

Each BibTeXEntry is transformed into a Para.

Each TitledEntry is transformed into a Para.

Each Article is transformed into a Para.

The References List must contain all bibliography entries as paragraphs ordered by identifier. Each entry paragraph must concatenate the identifier, the entry type, and every available value among title, authors, year, booktitle, school, journal, note, publisher, and chapter.

The Authors list must contain one paragraph for each distinct Author and sort the paragraphs by author name. The Titles List must contain one paragraph for each distinct titled entry and sort them by title. The Journals List must contain one paragraph for each distinct Article journal and sort them by journal name.

Generate the appropriate entry, title, and journal paragraphs according to whether an entry is a TitledEntry or an Article and whether its title or journal is present. Reuse the corresponding generated paragraphs when assembling the four sections, rather than creating inconsistent duplicates.
